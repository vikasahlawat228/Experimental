# concepts/

Mental models, processes, and ideas I want to think clearly about. The things that are not a specific service or person but are part of how Google or my team works.

Examples of what belongs here: "How does the launch process actually work." "What does TI mean and when do I need one." "How does our auth model differ from the public one." "What's the difference between a CL and a PR in our workflow."

File naming: kebab-case, describing the concept. `launch-process.md`, `what-is-a-ti.md`, `our-auth-model.md`.

These notes tend to be the most useful long-term. They are where my understanding of how things actually work gets clearer over time. Date them aggressively; come back and edit when you learn better.
