# research-dump/

Curated research from the `start-learning` skill — not a raw firehose. 6–10 named source files that earned their place, plus an INDEX with the rejection list and open questions.

## Naming

kebab-case, descriptive. `architecture-overview.md`, `original-design-doc-summary.md`, `2024-postmortem-walkthrough.md`. Not `source1.md`.

## What earns a spot here

- It's a primary source or a strong summary of one.
- It captures *why* a decision was made, not just *what* exists.
- It's an illustration or diagram worth re-reading.
- It's a real incident or case study (not generic).
- It's a specific person's writeup that I should mine in conversation later.

## What doesn't

- Generic blog posts that restate fundamentals.
- Anything I can find in 30 seconds via search.
- Marketing pages.
- Anything in the rejection list of `INDEX.md`.

`daily-learning` will draw from this dump but **always does its own narrow re-research per module** — the dump is the topic-wide context, not the per-session context.
