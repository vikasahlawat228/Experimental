# teams/

Other teams I work with or depend on. Light writeups, not org charts.

File naming: kebab-case team name. `ads-platform.md`, `infra-storage.md`.

Suggested sections:

```
# <team-name>

> As of YYYY-MM-DD: <What they own, what they care about, our relationship to them in one sentence>.

## What they own
## What they care about right now (priorities, current quarter)
## How we depend on them / how they depend on us
## Key contacts (link to people/ notes where they exist)
## Recent interactions worth remembering
## Open questions
```

Tip: when you write a teams note, also drop a one-line entry into any related people notes referencing it, and vice versa. Bidirectional links are what make this useful at 3am when you're trying to remember who-owns-what.
